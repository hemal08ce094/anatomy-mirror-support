# Anatomy Mirror support

Anatomy Mirror turns your Mac's camera into an anatomy mirror: a skull, brain, muscles and eyes drawn over your own face, following you as you move.

## Getting help

Open an issue on this page: https://github.com/hemal08ce094/anatomy-mirror-support/issues

Say which Mac and macOS version you use and what you saw. Do not attach video of yourself unless you want to.

## Common questions

**The camera view is blank.** Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Camera, and turn on Anatomy Mirror. If another app is using the camera, close it.

**The skull does not line up with my face.** Sit about an arm's length from the camera with light on your face. Then open Fit in the inspector and adjust Overall size, Height, Tilt and the position sliders. Reset Fit puts everything back.

**Can I use it without a camera?** Yes. Choose View, then Specimen Mode, and turn the model by dragging.

**Is this a scan of me?** No. The anatomy is a 3D model of one adult, scaled and pinned to your head. It shows where structures typically sit. It is not a medical tool.

**Where does my video go?** Nowhere. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
