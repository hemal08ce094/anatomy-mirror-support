# Anatomy Mirror privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Anatomy Mirror does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## Camera

The app uses your Mac's camera to find your face and hands so it can draw anatomy over them. Each video frame is analysed on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework and then discarded. Video is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The app makes no network connections.

## Saved plates

When you choose Save Plate, Copy or Share, the app creates an image of the current view. That image goes only where you send it. The app keeps no copy.

## Settings

Your layer, look and fit settings are stored on your Mac in the app's own preferences. Deleting the app removes them.

## Analytics, ads and accounts

There are none. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account.

## Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/hemal08ce094/anatomy-mirror-support/issues

## Anatomy data

Anatomy models are derived from BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International. The models show where structures typically sit. They are not a scan of you, and the app is not a medical tool.
